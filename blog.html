The Gardener, the Almanac & the Arborist

The garden is yours. No one else lives in your body, and no one else can do its daily tending — that responsibility, and that privilege, belongs to you. You are the gardener. A symptom is not the problem; it’s the garden asking for attention. House of Figs is your almanac — here to help you decode what the garden is saying and make the tending practical: simple steps, honest rhythms, discipline that pays you back in ways you can feel. That’s the wealth of wellness — not a quick fix, but a garden that holds. And your doctor is your arborist: your greatest asset for everything beyond the gardener’s reach — the things you can’t see, can’t understand, or can’t answer from inside the garden. Tend daily, so that when you call on them, you bring a clear picture too. The almanac makes you a better steward; the arborist remains the professional and the official seal of medical treatment. Together, that’s how things get to the root instead of staying stuck at the surface.
