# The Day-30 Look-Back — Conversation Guide

For Bethany, to use live during the call. This is a companion to the
[offer sheet](offer.html) and the funnel emails (C3, C4, C5) — the guide
is the conversation itself; the offer sheet is what you have open beside
you.

**When it happens:** booked at session four of The Rooted Beginning, then
confirmed by email C3 five days out. This is the moment the client
chooses how — or whether — to keep walking with House of Figs.

---

## Before the call

- Pull up their **Month in Review** in Fig·atry. You're not grading it —
  you're looking for the shape of the month: which colors actually
  showed up, which days the pour happened, where the gaps cluster.
- Skim the four weekly notes you sent them. What did you notice building
  over the month? Is there a thread from Week 1 to Week 4 worth naming
  out loud?
- Re-read their original goal, in their own words, from the prep sheet.
  Thirty days in, has it moved, sharpened, or stayed exactly the same?
  All three are worth mentioning.

## Opening the call

Warm, unhurried, no agenda stated up front. Something like:

> "Before we get into anything — how are you feeling, actually, right
> now, today?"

Let that answer breathe before moving to the structured part. The C3
email already asked them to look at their own Month in Review and come
with three things in mind — so they're expecting where this goes next.

## The three reflections

These are the exact three the email promised. Ask them in this order;
each one earns the next.

**1. What changed.**
Not "did you lose weight" — energy, sleep, digestion, mood, the small
harvests. If they don't have language for it yet, offer some: *steadier,
calmer, less foggy, fewer aches.* Let them correct you if that's not
quite it.

**2. What was hard.**
This is the one people rush past. Sit in it a beat longer than feels
natural. A hard month with real effort behind it is not a failed month —
say that plainly if it's true. Listen for whether "hard" means the food,
the time, or something underneath both.

**3. What their body seems to be asking for next.**
This is the pivot into the paths. Don't answer it for them yet — let
them take a first pass, even a rough one. It usually points toward one
of the three paths before you've said a single price out loud.

Somewhere in this conversation, one thing will land — a specific,
concrete win in their own words. Hold onto it. It's what C4 quotes back
to them by name (*"[the harvest] is exactly the kind of thing that
lasts"*), and it's the difference between a form email and one that
proves you were actually listening.

## Walking the paths

Once they've named what they're asking for, the paths mostly sort
themselves. Bring up the offer sheet together rather than reciting
figures from memory — it carries the full detail so you don't have to.

A rough fit, not a script:

- **Wants to keep talking it through** → Rooted Rhythms.
- **Knows the way, wants a lighter touch** → Fig&middot;atry Coached.
- **Already knows they want the full arc** → Rooted Season.
- **A doctor conversation is underway, or movement wants to enter now** →
  Deeper Roots.
- **Wants to go it alone with better tools** → Fig&middot;atry Plus, which
  is always there regardless of what else they choose.

Never lead with price. Lead with which season they're actually in, then
let the offer sheet answer the "what does it cost me" question in its own
plain language.

**If Deeper Roots comes up and labs are still pending:** that's fine and
expected — say so. The path can be chosen today and started the moment
the results are in; nothing about the timing needs to be a sticking
point in this call.

## If they're not ready to decide

That's a real, whole answer — treat it as one, not as a stall to push
past. Something like:

> "That's completely fine. Nothing has to be decided today. The three
> paths will still be here, and so will Fig&middot;atry Plus if you want
> to keep the plan and the logs working for you in the meantime."

This is exactly what C5 will reinforce a week later if nothing's been
chosen by then — one gentle reminder, then the door stays open without
more knocking.

## A safety check-in, every time

Before closing, ask plainly whether anything from the month is worth
bringing to their doctor — new symptoms, a medication change, anything
that surfaced in the Going Deeper form that's still unresolved. This
isn't a formality; it's the same principle that governs everything else
here: protecting them comes before the sale.

## Right after the call

- Write down the small harvest in their exact words while it's fresh —
  you'll want it verbatim for C4.
- Note which path they chose (or that they didn't choose one yet).
  **There's no admin field for this yet** — until the Day-30 tooling is
  built, jot it wherever you're tracking the client today, the same way
  you would before any of this was automated.
- Send C4 the same day if a path was chosen, with their checkout link
  from the offer sheet. If none was chosen, nothing sends until C5 at
  day 37 — no need to follow up sooner.
- If Deeper Roots was chosen with labs pending, let them know you're
  ready the moment the results land — no need to re-open the
  conversation when they do.

## The spirit of it

Everything else in this system — the quiz, the plan, the weekly notes —
is built to get someone here able to answer these three questions
honestly. The look-back isn't a sales call wearing a coaching hat; it's
the actual point of the thirty days; asking someone to notice what
changed. Whatever they choose next is a good choice, including choosing
to pause.
