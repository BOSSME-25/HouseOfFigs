Our offerings — and what each one asks of you.

A note before the menu

This is not a diet. It is a way back to yourself.

Every offering here begins the same way: with your story, read carefully, before we ever talk. From there we let your body point the way, one color at a time, one small doable shift on top of the last, until by day thirty the changes feel like rhythms rather than rules. Nothing below asks you to count, restrict, or perform. Everything below asks you to keep showing up, and gives you someone to show up with.

Each offering tells you plainly what it asks of your time, what it gives you of mine, and what it costs. Choose the one that fits the season you are actually in.

A few honest notes

What you can count on

  • This work walks alongside the care of your doctor; it does not replace it. Keep taking what they've prescribed, keep your appointments, and bring anything this raises to them and to me. If something in your story needs a doctor's eyes before we begin, I'll say so kindly and we'll wait. Protecting you comes before anything else.
  • You will not find calorie counts, weigh-ins, or appearance goals in anything I build for you. We measure by steadier energy, calmer days, better sleep, fewer aches, and the small harvests you start to notice for yourself.
  • Your information is yours. What you log in Fig·atry stays on your device unless you choose to share it with me, and you can export or erase it at any time.
  • If, after you've paid, your story turns out to need a doctor's eyes before a plan can be built, you are refunded in full. If you come back with their clearance within ninety days, your payment simply carries forward and we pick up where we paused.
  • Every offering can be paused or ended when your season changes. Your plan and your logs stay with you either way.
